Frequently asked questions

Why choose rope access over scaffolding?

It is typically faster to mobilise, far less intrusive and well suited to façades, voids and structures where scaffolding is impractical or costly. The right method depends on your project, and we will advise honestly.

Will our building stay operational during the work?

In most cases, yes. Our small footprint allows occupants to continue as normal, and we plan exclusion zones and timing around your operations.

Do you work on residential and commercial property?

Yes. We assist homes, estates, offices, retail centres and industrial facilities, including exterior surface and paving cleaning at ground level.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp with photos and a short description. We are open around the clock for enquiries and will respond with next steps.

Safety

Nothing leaves the ground until it is safe.

Safety is not a department at M&P Rope Access. It is how every task begins, and how every task ends.

Redundancy in everything.

Rope access is built on the principle that no single failure should ever place a technician at risk. Working and back-up lines, certified equipment, thorough pre-use checks and a rehearsed rescue plan form the backbone of our method.
